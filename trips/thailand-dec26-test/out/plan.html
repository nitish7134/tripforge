Where to stay

Bangkok (nearest airport BKK, 28 km)

Sukhumvit (Nana, Asok, Thonglor). On the BTS line, rooftop bars and clubs, many Indian veg places around Soi 11. Best nightlife base and best NYE base.

Silom / Sathorn. Business district, good value, BTS and MRT, Patpong and Silom Soi 4 nightlife.

Khao San / Old City. Backpacker strip, close to Grand Palace, noisy and basic.

See / do: Grand Palace, Pavilion of Regalia, Royal Decorations and Thai Coins, Wat Ratchabophit, Wat Ratchanaddaram, Wat Ratchapradit, Wat Saket and the Golden Mount, Wat Suthat and the Giant Swing, Wat Thep Thida Ram

Veg food: Khana Khazana, Indus

Nightlife: Cafe Velodrome, Foxhole, Jedi, Krisa, Rub Aroon, Wat Saket and Golden Mount, Amorosa Bar and The Deck, Angel Witch

Places from OpenStreetMap Overpass (unavailable), Wikivoyage (ok).

Koh Phangan (nearest airport URT, 118 km)

Haad Rin. Full Moon Party beach and the island's busiest bar strip. Loud; best base for party nights.

Thong Sala. Ferry pier town with the night market and most veg food. Cheaper, central, 20 min to Haad Rin.

Srithanu / Chaloklum. Yoga and vegan cafe belt in the north-west. Quiet; take a taxi to parties.

See / do: Archery, Diving, Full Moon Party, Herbal Sauna, Hiking, Muay Thai, Watersports, Yoga

Places from OpenStreetMap Overpass (unavailable), Wikivoyage (ok).

Krabi

Ao Nang. Base for Railay and island boats. Beach strip has bars and Indian food. Quieter than Patong.

Railay. Car-free peninsula, longtail boat from Ao Nang. Stay here only for a quiet beach trip.

See / do: Fossil Shell Beach, Rai Leh, Ko Phi Phi, Lagoon & Viewpoint, Phra Nang Cave, Phra Nang Shrine, Ao Nang Beach, Nopparat Thara Beach

Nightlife: Rick's Bar, Sweet Bar, Ting Tong Bar, Coco's, Rai Lay Bay Resort Bar, Cocktail Bar, Last Bar
