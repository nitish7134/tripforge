## Key live findings (2 Nov 2026)
- Christmas-Eve demand is into **Phuket** (BLR→HKT ₹38k) not **Bangkok** (BLR→BKK ₹27k). Entering via Bangkok and leaving via Phuket is the cheapest 24 Dec–3 Jan shape (~₹70k flights).
- Samui → Bangkok on 2–3 Jan is **sold out**; any Gulf-island NYE must exit via Krabi/Surat Thani or on 31 Dec/1 Jan.
- Dates swing flights by ~₹30k pp: 13–20 Dec ₹42.8k, 20–27 Dec ₹52.8k (HKT), 24 Dec–3 Jan ₹61.6k (+hop), 28 Dec–4 Jan ₹48.5k (+hop), 9–16 Jan ₹30.2k.
- Live Airbnb per night (2 ppl, ≥4.6, ≥10 reviews): Bangkok ₹2.7–3.3k, Chiang Mai ₹2.9–3.2k, Pattaya ₹3.7–4.2k, Phuket ₹2.1–4.1k, Krabi ₹1.9–2.8k (private rooms ₹1.6–2.1k), Phangan Full Moon week ₹5.5k+ (Haad Rin ₹8k+).
